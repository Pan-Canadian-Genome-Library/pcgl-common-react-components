# Colors

Every value on this page is read from the Token Source, `@repo/ui/tokens`. The system has two layers:

- The **Brand Palette** holds the colours of the PCGL Brand Guidelines (v2.0).
- **Role Tokens** say what a colour is for. They're named after antd's tokens (`colorPrimary`, `colorErrorBg`) and, in Tailwind, the same names in kebab case (`primary`, `error-bg`). <i>Components use Role Tokens only, never a literal colour or a Brand Palette colour.</i>


## Brand Palette

Some tints are **decorative only**: don't use them for hover, active, selected or any other state. 

<BrandPalette />

> Cool Gray's two lightest tints are the shared primary background shades (`colorPrimaryBg` and `colorPrimaryBgHover`).

## Themes

A Theme is the primary and link colour of one Portal, with their hover, active and border shades. Everything else is shared by every Theme. 
 > 🧛 There is no dark mode yet.

Portals pass their Theme's primary colour to the header and footer `brand` prop, as below.

## Variants

Each role has shades for its states: base, hover, active, background and border. The primary shades are in the Themes above. The primary background shades (`colorPrimaryBg`, `colorPrimaryBgHover`) are shared by every Theme, so a selected item is a light grey in every Portal.

### Status Pairs
Use the following  combinations:

<StatusPairings />

> ```🦮 Success (2.27:1) and warning (1.90:1) are below 3:1 on white. ```
> 
> **Always state a status in text**; never rely on colour or an icon alone. 
> Write status text in `colorText` on the status background. 
> Only the info and error active shades reach 4.5:1 on their backgrounds; 
> the success and warning shades are for icons, borders and decoration, not text.

## Focus ring

Every focusable element shows a 2px outline with a 2px offset, in the Theme's primary colour (`--color-focus-ring` in Tailwind). 
On a surface where primary has less than 3:1 contrast, use white instead (`--color-focus-ring-contrast`). 
Black is not a step. Pick the step per surface when you write the component.
